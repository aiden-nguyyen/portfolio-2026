Colour Reasoning

The palette is built around a near-black and an off-white for a clean, gallery-like feel that keeps attention on the work. A single blue accent highlights the name and key links, while two greys handle captions and dividers. Replace the hex values below with your actual colours.

Font Reasoning

The site uses a single typeface, General Sans, across its full weight range. Large headings use the heavier weights (600–700) for impact, body text sits at Regular (400), and small labels like project tags use Regular or Medium. Using one family with varied weights keeps the design cohesive and modern.

Basic Brand Idea

What does the site do, or offer?

It is the personal portfolio of Aiden Nguyen, a visual and UX/UI designer. The homepage introduces Aiden, showcases recent projects, and gives visitors a way to get in touch. The branding takes on the professional, consise approach.

Who is the site for?

Potential clients, collaborators, and employers seeking specialties in UX/UI and visual design. They also are looking to understand Aiden's design style and see examples of past work.

Why might a visitor reach out?

The clear, confident layout and curated project grid are meant to build trust quickly and make it easy for someone with a project in mind to start a conversation.
